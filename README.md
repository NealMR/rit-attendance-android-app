# RIT Attendance Android App

A sleek, fast, and feature-rich Android companion app for RIT Attendance. Manage your classes, view your attendance history, and stay on top of your schedule with ease.

## Download

You can always download the latest version of the app directly from our releases page:

[![Download Latest Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_APK-brightgreen?style=for-the-badge&logo=android)](https://github.com/NealMR/rit-attendance-android-app/releases/latest)

## Features
- **Dynamic Widgets**: Track your attendance and upcoming classes right from your home screen.
- **Offline Mode**: View your downloaded attendance logs without internet.
- **Smart Analytics**: Real-time feedback on how many classes you need to attend (or can safely miss) to maintain your target!

## Built With
- **Kotlin & Jetpack Compose**
- **Retrofit & OkHttp**
- **Room Database**
- **Glance App Widgets**
